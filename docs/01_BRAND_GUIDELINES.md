# 01 · Brand identity — Celestial Dunes

## Unique identity
Arabic display **سَدِيمًا** (with correct tanween fath on final meem and following alif). English **SADEEMAN AI**. Arabic promise: **رحلتك أبعد من الخيال.** English: **Beyond Destinations. Beyond Expectations.**
Origin: *sadeem* / a nebula, evoking curiosity, far horizons and discovery. The brand spelling is creative; do not claim global name exclusivity or formal trademark clearance. Avoid writing سَدِيم or سديمَا when showing the official wordmark.

### Brand distinction
No inherited navy/ocean, cyan, teal, mint or 'living travel network' brand compositions. This is NOT a re-skin of MASAR. The image language is warm, star-lit topographic dunes and a sculptural orbital lantern, not technological oceanography or generic robot faces.

### Fonts
- Arabic body: **Alexandria**, fallback IBM Plex Sans Arabic, system-ui; body 16px / line-height 1.75.
- Arabic display: **Reem Kufi**, sparingly. (If loading fails fallback Alexandria Bold.)
- English body: **DM Sans**, system-ui, 16px / 1.55.
- English display: **Sora**, 600/700; use only for headlines/wordmark.
- Mixed text: Unicode direction isolation for URLs, currency and code. Preserve Arabic diacritics on logo; do not impose Latin all-caps transforms on Arabic.

### Visual language
- Orbit Lantern icon: three interrupted non-overlapping warm arcs enclosing a four-point star. Original SVG; no borrowed travel logo or airplane clipart.
- Panels: rounded 18–24px, soft shadow and low-noise matte surfaces. Glass only for overlays, never at the cost of reading.
- Motifs: sparse engraved topographic lines, distant gold constellation points, warm edge light and smooth orbital sculpture.
- Motion: responsive to TRUE runtime state. Idle breathing 12–18s; listening responds to captured audio level; searching shows genuine tool activity; speaking responds to playback state; stop on reduced motion.
- Light theme should feel like a premium journal in warm ivory, not simply inverted dark mode. Dark theme is intimate dusk purple, not navy.

### Copy and personality
Egyptian Arabic naturally warm and composed; user-led, not salesy. No AI-human impersonation, no emojis in every reply, no forced closing sales line. Arabic greeting concept: «أهلًا! تحب نبدأ من وجهة معينة ولا نختار رحلة على مزاجك؟» English: 'Where would you like to begin exploring?'

### UI labels
Statuses: «جاري البحث» only after a server tool event; «عرض تجريبي» for seeded company content; «التوافر غير مؤكد» for web listings; «تم إرسال الطلب» only after backend acknowledgment.
